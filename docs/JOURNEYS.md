# User journeys, screen by screen

## Hiker — Joana, beginner, wants a Saturday walk near Porto
| Step | Screen | What she does | What she sees | Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Landing (signed out) | Reads what TrailSense does, creates an account | Headline, 3 pillars, difference vs other apps, sources and limits | AC0.1 |
| 2 | Trails | Filters Region = Valongo, ≤ 3 h | Cards with photo, real map, PR code, km, time, ascent, difficulty | AC1.1–1.2, AC1.15, AC1.17 |
| 3 | Trail page | Opens Trilho dos Romanos; opens "Is this route for me?" and the weather dropdown | PR explained ("short route, one day"), climbs and profile; 7 days with rain chance and wind | AC1.13–1.16 |
| 4 | Trail page | Saves the trail, shares it with a friend, presses "Plan this day" | ★ Saved; link copied | AC1.18–1.19 |
| 5 | Plan form | Keeps the date, sets 09:30 and 2 h | Duration pre-filled from the trail | AC2.1–2.2 |
| 6 | Risk check | Reads the verdict | "Go prepared — rain expected", hour-by-hour chart, what to bring, rule behind each factor, link to raw data | AC4.1–4.15, AC5.1–5.2 |
| 7 | Hike page (Saturday, at home) | Downloads the trail for offline use | "✓ Ready offline" | AC13.12 |
| 8 | Hike page (on the trail) | Starts the hike, follows "In 70 m · turn left", checks the marks | Live position, compass, progress, next climb, off-route alert | AC13.1–13.11 |
| 9 | Finish → Publish | Publishes with a photo | Form pre-filled with distance and time; post on top of the feed | AC13.8, AC7.1–7.4 |
| 10 | Home | Sees her stats and the next plan | Hero with countdown and verdict, saved trails, recent hikes | AC8.3, AC11.1 |

## Organizer — Bruno, runs a hiking club
| Step | Screen | What he does | What he sees | Criteria |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Organizer dashboard | Presses "+ New group hike" | Events, sign-ups, places left, K1 | AC9.8, K1 |
| 2 | Plan form (group) | Chooses the trail, date, meeting point, 8 places | Group fields validated | AC9.1 |
| 3 | Event page | Shares the invite link | Risk check for the event, briefing, "Share invite link" | AC9.2–9.3, AC1.19 |
| 4 | (Hikers) Groups | Members join with one tap | Places left decrease; "Full" when full | AC9.4–9.5 |
| 5 | Dashboard | Follows sign-ups and the weather | 2/8 Open, verdict badge per event | AC9.8 |
| 6 | Edit event | Moves the start to 10:00 | Weather re-checked; cannot go below people already joined | AC9.9 |
| 7 | Event page | Copies the briefing to the club chat | Trail, meeting, timing, warnings and precautions in one text | AC9.2–9.3 |
| 8 | (If needed) Cancel | Cancels the event | Participants no longer see it | AC9.9 |
